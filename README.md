This project uses churn data for an e-commerce company to make predictions on whether or not a customer has continued their subscription. A Tableau dashboard using this data can be found here: https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/ethan.chu2920/viz/ECommerceChurnandRevenue/Dashboard1?publish=yes

Annual recurring revenue peaked at 2021 but saw a steady decline over the next few years. In addition, the number of active subscriptions mirrored that decline as annual recurring revenue is dependent on customers purchasing and renewing their subscriptions every year. This e-commerce company needs to understand why exactly they were able to attract someone people to purchase subscriptions in 2021 but why they saw a decrease in subscriptions after that year. This can be attributed to the service being offered by the subscription. If customers no longer find value in the subscription after a year, they will not choose to renew. The business must ensure that their services do not become obselete. This can be improved through improving upon what is currently offered in the subscription package or adjusting prices to match customer expectations. If I want to see this business continue growing year over year, I want to see indefinite expansion in product selection so that customers will have more reason to purchase a subscription and I need to see definitive value for customers to continue renewing their subscription every year.
